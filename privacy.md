# Samely — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 7, 2026**

## In short

Samely collects no data. Your photos, the analyses made from them and how you use the app are never sent off this phone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party software.

## Photo access

Your library is accessed only to find similar photos. A small copy of each photo is examined on the phone and a visual summary is made from it. These summaries are stored only in the app's own space, are not included in iCloud Backup, and are deleted when you delete the app.

## iCloud photos

If it's on in Settings, iOS downloads small versions of photos that have no copy on the phone from your iCloud Photos account, only while on Wi-Fi. Samely doesn't connect to any server of its own while this happens.

## Place names

Place names in group titles are looked up on the phone in a place list that comes with the app; a photo's location is never sent to any service. The place list is GeoNames data (geonames.org, CC BY 4.0).

## Deleting

Deleting is always done with iOS's own confirmation. Deleted photos stay in the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app for 30 days and can be restored from there.

## Diagnostics

To understand problems, the app keeps a small event log on the phone, along with iOS's performance measurements. The app sends these nowhere; they are shared only if you choose to, with "Share diagnostics log" in Settings. If "Share With App Developers" is on in iPhone Settings, Apple may share crash reports through its own system.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be here and on the App Store page.
