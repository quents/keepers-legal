# Samely — Support & Legal

Samely finds similar photos in your library on your iPhone, suggests the best
of each group, and lets you delete the rest. Everything happens on the phone.

## Support

Email **support@yaziciyusuf.com**. We usually reply within 1–2 business days.

### Common questions

- **Where do deleted photos go?** To Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where
  they stay for 30 days and can be restored.
- **Why is the scan not finished?** Samely looks at your newest photos right
  away and finishes the rest in the background while your iPhone charges.
- **How do I manage or cancel Monthly Pro?** Subscriptions are handled by
  Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Samely.
- **I bought Pro on another iPhone.** Open Samely Pro in the app and tap
  Restore Purchases.
